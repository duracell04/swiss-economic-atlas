# Real GDP per capita — verified source audit

Reviewed 12 October 2026. **Verified as a documented derived series; 1948–2025.**

The numerator is the FSO-derived chain-linked real GDP level. The denominator is the FSO permanent-resident population at 31 December (`POP_DEC`), which is already retained as a verified annual series. The atlas converts CHF millions to CHF per person and deliberately labels this as an end-of-period denominator; it does not imply an annual-average population estimate.

The calculation is registered as `per_capita` and regenerates from the real GDP and population inputs. The output inherits the GDP's 1995 accounting break and the population definition changes documented around 1981 and 2011. 2026 is absent because neither input supplies a completed-year value.
