# Confederation gross debt — verified source audit

Reviewed 12 October 2026. **Verified; 1990–2026 selected.**

The [FFA financial-statistics data catalogue](https://www.efv.admin.ch/de/finanzstatistik-daten) links the [FS/GFS main-aggregates record](https://opendata.swiss/en/dataset/hauptaggregate-und-prognosen-im-fs-und-gfs-modell), its [CSV resource](https://www.data.finance.admin.ch/static/assets/datasets/fs_dashboard/main_extern.csv) and the [dimension codelist](https://www.data.finance.admin.ch/static/assets/datasets/fs_dashboard/main_extern_codelist.csv). The atlas selects `hh=bund`, `model=fs`, `variable=bruttoschuld_fs`, in CHF millions.

Rows labelled `Rechnung` are imported as observed/final. The 2026 `Hochrechnung` is retained as forecast/provisional and is not presented as a completed-year observation. Federal gross debt is separate from consolidated general-government debt and from the GFS Maastricht series.

The source snapshot is immutable and the registry preserves the FFA attribution and OPEN reuse terms. Future FFA vintages require a new snapshot and a reviewed replacement import.
